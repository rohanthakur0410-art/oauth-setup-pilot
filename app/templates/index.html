Four dimensions, rated independently: technical setup difficulty · your hands-on time · whether approval is required, conditional, optional or not required · and the external wait. Approval that depends on scopes or distribution is rated conditional, never "free". Unknowns stay unknown.

Two steps people confuse

(1) You register one OAuth app with the provider and, often, get it approved or verified. (2) Each end user connects their account through a normal consent screen. Step 2 is quick everywhere; step 1 ranges from a 10-minute form (GitHub) to a weeks-long verification (Google restricted scopes). Every guide separates hands-on setup time from approval wait, names the exact console, and marks anything unverified as such.
